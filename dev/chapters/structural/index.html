Structural ID, LP & Innovation Accounting

Recovering structural economic shocks $\varepsilon_t$ from reduced-form residuals $u_t = B_0 \varepsilon_t$—and quantifying propagation via impulse response functions (irf), forecast error variance decompositions (fevd), and historical decompositions (hd)—connects reduced-form VARs/BVARs/VECMs/FAVARs/SDFMs/TVP-VARs, parametric SVAR/SVEC (estimate multivariate svar|svec), non-Gaussian ICA/ML (estimate factor fastica, estimate regression ml), and direct local projections (estimate multivariate lp). See MacroEconometricModels.jl — Structural Identification and Local Projections.

Quick Start

Estimate Cholesky-identified impulse responses with Kilian bias-corrected or iid bootstrap bands on :denmark, and decompose forecast error variance over 4 horizons:

Classical, Set-Identified & Narrative SVAR Identification

Because covariance symmetry $\Sigma_u = B_0 B_0'$ leaves $n(n-1)/2$ free parameters of an orthogonal rotation $Q$ ($QQ' = I_n$) undetermined, structural identification requires theory-guided restrictions passed via --id and (for matrices/inequalities/dates) --config (Priors, SVAR & VECM Identification):

\[u_t = B_0 \varepsilon_t, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon_t) = I_n, \qquad \Theta_h = \Phi_h B_0 Q.\]

Identification Strategy--id Value(s)Restriction Mechanism & Output Notes
Recursive & Long-Runcholesky, longrunImpact lower-triangular ($B_0 = \mathrm{chol}(\Sigma_u)$) or Blanchard–Quah long-run zeros (estimate multivariate svar patterns recursive, blanchard-quah, a-model, b-model, ab-model; cointegrated svec)
Sign & Narrativesign, narrative[identification.sign_matrix] signs on responses plus [identification.narrative] shock signs; add --identified-set + --summary (median-target|modal-model|joint-band|sup-t-band)
Zero + Sign & Penaltyarias, uhlig, narrative-adrrImportance sampling on $Q$ (Arias et al. 2018; ADRR narrative shares) or penalty optimization (Mountford–Uhlig 2009); emits arias_importance_sampling_diagnostics
External Instrument / Max-Share / Momentsproxy, max-share, gmm-momentsProxy SVAR via --instrument; Uhlig business-cycle variance share via --target-var; non-Gaussian skewness/kurtosis GMM
Robust Set-Identified BVARrobust-bayesGiacomini–Kitagawa multiple-prior bounds under irf bvar (robust_bayes_bands, robust_bayes_diagnostics)

Non-Gaussian ICA & Heteroskedastic SVARs

When structural innovations are mutually independent and at most one shock is Gaussian (or conditional volatility varies across regimes or stochastic volatility states), $B_0$ is point-identified up to column permutation and sign without zero/sign timing assumptions:

  • Non-Gaussian Independence & Parametric Non-Gaussian SVAR: Run estimate factor fastica (--method fastica|jade|sobi|dcov|hsic|cvm, including Cramér–von Mises cvm ICA, --contrast logcosh|exp|kurtosis), parametric skewed/heavy-tailed MLE estimate regression ml (--distribution student_t|mixture_normal|pml|skew_normal), Lanne–Meitz–Saikkonen (2017) per-shock distributional MLE estimate multivariate lms-svar (--distributions), Lanne–Luoto co-kurtosis GMM estimate multivariate lanne-luoto, or structural SVARMA estimate multivariate ssvarma (--blaschke non-fundamental roots; paired with irf ssvarma and fevd ssvarma). Pass --id fastica|jade|sobi|dcov|hsic|cvm|student_t|mixture_normal|pml|skew_normal directly to irf/fevd/hd.
  • SVAR Hypothesis, Fundamentalness & Non-Gaussian Diagnostics: Test 7 structural restriction classes (recursiveness, impact_zero, impact_linear, long_run, horizon_irf, mixed, plus parametric invariance) with test multivariate svar-hypothesis ([svar_hypothesis] in Priors, SVAR & VECM Identification), verify residual independence with test multivariate nongaussian-diag and test multivariate gmm-moments, and test VAR invertibility / informational sufficiency via test multivariate fundamentalness and test multivariate sufficient-info.
  • Dynamic Factor & Frequency-Domain Structural Accounting: Estimate one-sided FHLZ dynamic factors (estimate factor fhlz) or $I(1)$ permanent-transitory GDFMs (estimate factor gdfm-i1, paired with irf gdfm-i1, fevd gdfm-i1, and hd gdfm-i1), and compute band-specific frequency-domain variance decompositions with spectral fevd.
  • Heteroskedasticity Identification: Pass --id markov_switching, --id garch_id, --id lewis-tvv (Lewis 2021 GMM via time-varying volatility), or --id sv-em (Bertsche–Braun stochastic volatility EM; config schema in Priors, SVAR & VECM Identification).

Local Projections (estimate multivariate lp, irf/fevd/hd lp)

Jordà (2005) local projections estimate dynamic causal responses horizon-by-horizon via direct regressions of $y_{t+h}$ on the shock/instrument and control lags, avoiding VAR lag misspecification:

\[y_{t+h} = \alpha_h + \beta_h \, s_t + \gamma_h' W_{t-1} + u_{t+h}, \qquad h = 0, \dots, H.\]

estimate multivariate lp supports six variants via --method: standard, iv (external instrument LP-IV with --instruments, Montiel Olea–Pflueger effective F --mop-f, and weak-IV Anderson–Rubin bands --ar-bands), penalized smooth B-splines (--knots, --lambda), Auerbach–Gorodnichenko state-dependent logistic transitions (--state-var, --gamma), inverse-probability propensity weighting, and doubly robust AIPW estimators. Follow up directly with irf lp, fevd lp, hd lp, or forecast multivariate lp.

Common Pitfalls

  1. Passing --ci bootstrap together with --id arias|uhlig|narrative-adrr or --identified-set: Those set-identified Bayesian/importance samplers manage posterior bands internally, so bootstrap flags (--ci, --bootstrap, --bias-correct, --cumulative) are ignored there.
  2. Omitting --instrument (proxy) or --target-var (max-share): irf var requires --instrument pointing to the external instrument column when --id proxy is active, and --target-var when --id max-share is selected.
  3. Weak instruments in LP-IV (--method iv): Always enable --mop-f and --ar-bands on estimate multivariate lp (montiel_olea_pflueger_effective_f, lp_iv_anderson_rubin_bands) whenever external macro instruments have borderline first-stage power.
