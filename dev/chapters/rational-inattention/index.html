Rational Inattention

Information-constrained decision making—covering linear-quadratic-Gaussian (LQG) dynamic tracking, finite-horizon attention transitions, state-space & structural estimation, Coibion–Gorodnichenko expectation-rigidity tests, Maćkowiak–Wiederholt multi-block attention allocation, Matějka–McKay discrete choice, and structural RI-logit—lives across the 11 leaves under frdc ri together with estimate choice ri-logit and predict choice ri-logit. Macro RI estimation and expectation-rigidity leaves (ri estimate, ri cg-test) default to 68% confidence intervals (-L/--conf-level 0.68), while discrete-choice estimate choice ri-logit defaults to 90%. Configure [ri], [ri.mw], [ri.discrete], and [ri.logit] TOML sections via GMM, SMM, Systems, State-Space & Policy. See MacroEconometricModels.jl — Rational Inattention for full derivations.

Quick Start

Define a scalar LQG tracking problem (x_t = 0.85 x_{t-1} + 0.5 \varepsilon_t) under a mutual-information constraint of capacity = 0.5 bits per period, solve the optimal Sims–Kalman noisy signal structure with frdc ri solve, save the solved model handle, and inspect the implied impulse responses:

<!– capture –>

cat > ri_lqg.toml <<'EOF'
[ri]
A = [[0.85]]
B = [[0.5]]
H = [[1.0]]
beta = 0.99
capacity = 0.5
units = "bits"
state_names = ["x"]
action_names = ["a"]
shock_names = ["eps"]

Saving --save-model ri_sol persists a native SimsKalmanSolution .jld2 handle that feeds ri irf, ri fevd, ri statespace, ri transition, ri simulate, and ri cg-test without re-solving.

LQG Dynamic Tracking & Structural Estimation (solve, transition, statespace, estimate)

In the Sims (2003) / Afrouzi–Yang (2021) dynamic LQG problem, an agent tracks a latent Gaussian state $x_t = A x_{t-1} + B \varepsilon_t$ with optimal target $a_t^* = H' x_t$, trading off quadratic tracking loss against Shannon mutual-information flow $\mathcal{I}(x_t; s_t \mid s^{t-1})$ priced at shadow cost $\omega > 0$ (or capped at capacity $\kappa > 0$):

\[\min_{\{s_t, a_t\}} \mathbb{E}_0 \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \beta^t \left[ (a_t - H' x_t)' M (a_t - H' x_t) + \omega \, \mathcal{I}(x_t; s_t \mid s^{t-1}) \right].\]

CommandMathematical RoleKey Outputs (--format json)
ri solveSteady-state Sims–Kalman water-filling (--method afrouzi-yang|reverse-waterfilling, --capacity or --omega)ri_kalman_solution, ri_summary (or ri_problem under --problem-only)
ri transitionFinite-horizon attention dynamics from Sigma_init or time-varying omega_path/capacity_path (--t-path)ri_transition_path, ri_transition_summary
ri statespaceConvert solved RI dynamics into a canonical StateSpaceModel (--as statespace) or ABCDSystem (--as abcd)ri_statespace_system / abcd_summary
ri irf, ri fevdStructural action/state impulse responses and forecast-error variance shares (--horizons, --observables)ri_irf, ri_fevd
ri simulateSeeded synthetic state, signal, and action series (--periods, --burn, --save-data)ri_simulation
ri estimateEstimate $\kappa$ or $\omega$ via state-space MLE or Bayesian MH (--method mle|bayes, --estimate-param capacity|omega)ri_estimation, ri_estimation_summary

Notice that every solved LQG object converts cleanly into an econometric state-space or canonical ABCD representation via ri statespace, letting you test invertibility and shock recoverability with dsge abcd (Representative-Agent & Bayesian DSGE) or pass RI attention gains (--behavioral-ri-gain, --behavioral-ri-rho) into policy news dsge (Policy Counterfactuals & Optimal Policy).

Multi-Block Attention Allocation & Coibion–Gorodnichenko Tests (mw, cg-test)

When firms allocate a scarce total attention budget $\kappa = \kappa_{\text{agg}} + \kappa_{\text{idio}}$ across aggregate macro shocks and larger, less persistent idiosyncratic shocks (Maćkowiak & Wiederholt 2009), optimal pricing places most attention on idiosyncratic conditions and responds sluggishly to monetary shocks—generating positive Coibion–Gorodnichenko (2015) forecast-error-on-forecast-revision regressions:

\[y_{t+h} - F_t y_{t+h} = \alpha + \beta_{\text{CG}} \left( F_t y_{t+h} - F_{t-1} y_{t+h} \right) + u_{t+h}, \qquad \beta_{\text{CG}} = \frac{1 - G}{G}.\]

  • Aggregate vs. Idiosyncratic Attention (ri mw): Solves optimal block attention shares (kappa_agg, kappa_idio, agg_attention_share) with optional --strategic-complementarity feedback under [ri.mw.agg_block] and [ri.mw.idio_block].
  • Expectations Rigidity Test (ri cg-test): Regresses forecast errors on forecast revisions with Newey–West HAC standard errors (--realized, --forecast-now, --forecast-lag, --hac-lags), reporting the implied Kalman gain $G$ and information capacity; pass --model ri_sol.jld2 to compare the empirical slope directly against model_implied_cg_slope.

<!– capture –>

cat > ri_mw.toml <<'EOF'

capacity = 2.0

[ri.mw.agg_block]
rho = 0.90

[ri.mw.idio_block]

EOF

Discrete-Choice Rational Inattention (discrete, iia-test, ri-logit)

In Matějka & McKay (2015), a rationally inattentive agent choosing among $J$ discrete options across $S$ payoff states with prior $\mu$ solves the Blahut–Arimoto / generalized multinomial-logit fixed point:

\[P(a = j \mid s) = \frac{P_0(j) \exp\left(u(j, s) / \omega\right)}{\sum_{k=1}^J P_0(k) \exp\left(u(k, s) / \omega\right)}, \qquad P_0(j) = \sum_{s=1}^S \mu(s) P(a = j \mid s),\]

where endogenous unconditional choice weights $P_0(j)$ can shrink to zero (forming a sparse consideration set) and violate McFadden's Independence of Irrelevant Alternatives (IIA) whenever correlated alternatives enter the choice menu.

  • Equilibrium Choice Probabilities (ri discrete): Solves unconditional and state-conditional choice probabilities, mutual information, expected utility, net welfare, and active consideration_set from [ri.discrete] (U, mu, plus --omega or --capacity).
  • IIA Violation Diagnostic (ri iia-test): Compares base choices against an expanded menu with new_option_payoffs, reporting max_uncond_odds_shift, cond_odds_ratio_max_diff, and max_iia_violation.
  • Empirical Structural RI-Logit (estimate choice ri-logit, predict choice ri-logit): Estimates preference weights and attention-cost ratios directly from observed multi-environment choice panels (both observed-state and --unobserved-state mixtures; see also Discrete Choice, Count & Selection).

<!– capture –>

cat > ri_disc.toml <<'EOF'
[ri.discrete]
U = [[2.0, 0.0], [0.0, 2.0], [0.5, 0.5]]
mu = [0.5, 0.5]

Common Pitfalls

  1. Passing both --capacity and --omega (or setting both in [ri]): Information capacity $\kappa$ and shadow cost $\omega$ are dual parameterizations and strictly mutually exclusive; supplying both raises exit 2 (usage/invalid) or 4 (config/invalid).
  2. Mixing bits and nats when comparing capacities: Specify --units bits (base-2 log, default in Sims / Maćkowiak–Wiederholt) or --units nats (natural log, standard in Matějka–McKay discrete choice) consistently across [ri] configs and CLI overrides; summary tables report both capacity_bits and capacity_nats.
  3. Expecting --plot on ri simulate: All 10 analytical/diagnostic ri leaves (solve, transition, statespace, irf, fevd, estimate, cg-test, mw, discrete, iia-test) plus estimate choice ri-logit support --plot / --plot-save; ri simulate emits raw simulated paths (--save-data) and intentionally omits --plot.
