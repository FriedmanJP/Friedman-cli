Representative-Agent & Bayesian DSGE

Representative-agent dynamic stochastic general equilibrium solving, deterministic transition paths, occasionally binding constraints (OccBin), parameter estimation, and full Bayesian SMC / Random-Walk Metropolis–Hastings inference live across the representative-agent leaves under friedman dsge (ra family, ref-fam-dsge-ra). Heterogeneous-agent, continuous-time, OLG, DCEGM, firm, and banking equilibrium models (hadsge and dsge ct|olg|dcegm|lifecycle|firm|bank) are covered separately in Heterogeneous-Agent, CT & OLG Models. See MacroEconometricModels.jl — DSGE.

Quick Start

Write an @dsge begin ... end .jl model file (or TOML [model] spec in DSGE Specs, Priors & OccBin Constraints), solve linear rational expectations via Sims (gensys), and inspect policy and transition matrices:

<!– capture –>

cat > rbc_lin.jl <<'EOF'
@dsge begin
    parameters: rho = 0.85, sigma = 0.02, kappa = 0.25
    endogenous: y, pi
    exogenous: e_a
    linear: true

    y[t] = rho * y[t-1] + sigma * e_a[t]
    pi[t] = 0.99 * pi[t+1] + kappa * y[t]
end
EOF
friedman dsge solve rbc_lin.jl --method gensys --periods 6

Steady States, Perturbation, Projection & OccBin (solve, irf, perfect-foresight)

A nonlinear rational-expectations equilibrium satisfies conditional Euler and market-clearing conditions around deterministic steady state $\bar{y}$:

\[\mathbb{E}_t\left[f(y_{t+1}, y_t, y_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t; \theta)\right] = 0, \qquad \varepsilon_t \sim (0, \Sigma_\varepsilon).\]

Follow dsge steady-state directly with dsge solve or pass --save-model so dsge irf and dsge simulate reuse the solved policy functions. Notice --constraint 'i[t] >= 0.0' (one-sided) activates OccBin regime switching in solve and irf, while perfect-foresight and steady-state also support two-sided bounds lo <= x[t] <= hi.

<!– capture –>

cat > nk_occ.jl <<'EOF'
@dsge begin
    parameters: rho = 0.8, sigma = 0.01
    endogenous: y, c
    exogenous: e
    linear: true

    y[t] = rho * y[t-1] + sigma * e[t]
    c[t] = y[t]
end
EOF

Classical & Bayesian DSGE Estimation (estimate, dsge bayes *)

Friedman-cli estimates structural parameters $\theta$ by matching impulse responses, moments, or state-space Gaussian likelihood $p(Y_{1:T} \mid \theta) p(\theta)$ using Sequential Monte Carlo (--sampler smc|smc2) or Random-Walk Metropolis–Hastings (--sampler mh).

  • Frequentist Structural Estimation (dsge estimate): Choose --method irf_matching|euler_gmm|smm|analytical_gmm over observable --data, listing calibrated/estimated symbols in --params (dsge_estimation).
  • Bayesian Workflow (dsge bayes): Write priors in a priors: preamble above @dsge, repeat --prior 'rho ~ beta(2, 2)', or supply a [priors.*] TOML file (DSGE Specs, Priors & OccBin Constraints), choosing observational transforms (--prefilter none|demean|first-difference|linear-detrend|hp) and measurement noise (--measurement-error).

Common Pitfalls

  1. Passing --config, mixing priors: with constraints:, or putting priors: on bayes identification: No dsge leaf takes --config. Place priors above @dsge (for sampling commands such as prior-predictive, posterior-mode, and estimate) or pass repeatable --prior/--constraint flags and --priors/--constraints TOML files—never combine priors: and constraints: in one .jl file, and omit priors: when running dsge bayes identification (DSGE Specs, Priors & OccBin Constraints).
  2. Omitting [t] timing or writing E[t](...): Bare symbols or (+1)/(-1) syntax trigger config/invalid (exit 4); index every endogenous variable by [t], [t-1], or [t+1] and exogenous shocks strictly by [t].
  3. Stochastic singularity (model/stochastic-singularity, exit 5) in Bayesian estimation: When --observables lists more data series than structural shocks in exogenous:, either add measurement error (--measurement-error auto / explicit values) or reduce the observable subset.
