Choosing a Method

Match your data structure, identification assumptions, and research question to the right Friedman-cli verb family and workflow chapter.

By Data Type

Each estimator family accepts specific container kinds enforced at dispatch (returning data/wrong-kind, exit code 3, on mismatch):

Data StructureHandle KindTypical Frequency / DimensionsPrimary Chapters
Single macro/financial series:timeseries (N = 1)Monthly, quarterly, daily returnsUnivariate, Filters & Spectral, Volatility & Multivariate GARCH, Regime-Switching & Nonlinear TS
Multiple aggregate series:timeseries (N > 1)Quarterly/monthly macro blocks; mixed-freqVAR, BVAR, VECM & Factor Models, Structural ID, LP & Innovation Accounting, Nowcasting & Forecast Evaluation
Unit-by-time observations:panelBalanced or unbalanced (group, time)Panel Data, Panel VAR & Causal DiD
Independent observations:cross_sectionHousehold/firm survey or experimental unitsRegression, Systems & GMM/SMM, Discrete Choice, Count & Selection
Economic model (no raw data needed for solve)Model spec (.jl / .toml)Structural calibration or Bayesian estimationRepresentative-Agent & Bayesian DSGE, Heterogeneous-Agent, CT & OLG Models, Policy Counterfactuals & Optimal Policy
Inter-sector flows / weightsIO / BF .toml or .csvSector × sector intermediate matrixInput-Output & Production Networks

Time Series & Structural Macro

  1. Need trend/cycle decomposition or frequency-domain structure? Start in Univariate, Filters & Spectral (filter hp|hamilton|bn|bk|bhp|x13, spectral acf|periodogram|density|cross|transfer, estimate univariate arima|sarima|arfima|ardl|nardl|midas).
  2. Modeling time-varying volatility or dynamic correlation? Go to Volatility & Multivariate GARCH for ARCH/GARCH/EGARCH/GJR/APARCH/CGARCH/FIGARCH/IGARCH, stochastic volatility (sv), GARCH-MIDAS (garch-midas), and multivariate GARCH (ccc, dcc, bekk).
  3. Expecting discrete regime switches or smooth threshold nonlinearity? Use Regime-Switching & Nonlinear TS (estimate regime threshold|setar|star|ms-ar|ms, paired with Hansen/STAR linearity tests).
  4. Forecasting or decomposing multi-variable dynamics? Fit reduced-form VAR, Minnesota/diffuse BVAR, VECM, mixed-frequency VAR, TVP-VAR, FAVAR, or dynamic factor models in VAR, BVAR, VECM & Factor Models.
  5. Recovering structural shocks, IRFs, FEVDs, or historical decompositions? Combine Cholesky, long-run, sign/narrative/Arias/Uhlig, proxy IV, max-share, non-Gaussian ICA, or heteroskedastic/SV identification with LP or VAR/BVAR/VECM/FAVAR/TVP-VAR in Structural ID, LP & Innovation Accounting.
  6. Nowcasting ragged-edge quarterly GDP from monthly releases or comparing forecasts? Use nowcast and forecast evaluate in Nowcasting & Forecast Evaluation.

Microeconometrics, Panel & Causal Inference

  • Continuous outcomes, endogeneity, shrinkage, semiparametric, or structural moments: Regression, Systems & GMM/SMM covers OLS, IV/2SLS, quantile, sharp/fuzzy RDD, Ridge/Lasso/Elastic Net, Huber/MM robust, FMOLS/DOLS/CCR cointegrating regression, SUR/3SLS, GMM, SMM, and KDE/LOESS.
  • Discrete, ordered, count, censored, or sample-selected outcomes: Discrete Choice, Count & Selection covers binary/ordered/multinomial logit & probit, Poisson/negative binomial, Tobit, truncated regression, and Heckman two-step/MLE.
  • Longitudinal & difference-in-differences identification: Panel Data, Panel VAR & Causal DiD provides static/dynamic FE/RE/FD/Hausman-Taylor, panel logit/probit/Tobit/PMG, panel VAR (pvar + GMM/IRF/FEVD/stability/Andrews-Lu tests), staggered Callaway-Sant'Anna / Sun-Abraham / BJS / Wooldridge / stacked DiD, event studies, HonestDiD sensitivity, and lp-did.

Structural Equilibrium, Policy & Networks

  • Representative-agent equilibrium: Representative-Agent & Bayesian DSGE solves linearized, higher-order pruned, projection, perfect-foresight, and inequality-constrained (bounds/OccBin) DSGE models and estimates parameters via SMC or Random-Walk Metropolis-Hastings.
  • Distributional & continuous-time equilibrium: Heterogeneous-Agent, CT & OLG Models handles sequence-space HouseholdSystem models (hadsge), Huggett/Aiyagari/Krusell-Smith/HANK (dsge ct), OLG (dsge olg), discrete-continuous choice (dsge dcegm|lifecycle), firm dynamics (dsge firm), and banking (dsge bank).
  • Counterfactuals & normative central banking: Policy Counterfactuals & Optimal Policy runs zeroing/sequence counterfactuals, policy rules, Ramsey/timeless optimal policy, and commitment vs. discretion comparisons.
  • Sectoral propagation: Input-Output & Production Networks computes Leontief/Ghosh multipliers, hypothetical extraction, structural decomposition analysis, and Baqaee-Farhi nonlinear general equilibrium counterfactuals.

Diagnostic Decision Map

Unsure which specification test checks unit roots, cointegration rank, lag misspecification, structural breaks, or DiD pre-trends? Use the master test decision matrix in Hypothesis Testing & Diagnostics.
