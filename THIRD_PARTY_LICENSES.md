## Core GPL-3.0-only Compatible Dependency Stack

- **Built-in NLP Solver:** `MadNLP` (`0.8.x`, MIT) + `NLPModels` (MPL-2.0) + `LDLFactorizations` (LGPL-2.1+ / MPL-2.0) + `SuiteSparse` (GPL-2.0+) + `OpenBLAS` (BSD-3-Clause).
- **Non-MKL Preferences:** `LocalPreferences.toml` pins `[LinearSolve] LoadMKL_JLL = false` and `[FFTW] provider = "fftw"`, ensuring proprietary Intel MKL (`MKL_jll`), `oneTBB_jll`, and `IntelOpenMP_jll` are never loaded or bundled.
- **Excluded Weak Dependencies:** `Ipopt` (`EPL-2.0` + `MUMPS_seq_jll` `CeCILL-C`) and `PATHSolver` (proprietary) are isolated as optional upstream `[weakdeps]` and are never resolved into `Manifest.toml` or bundled in distributed binaries.
