Heterogeneous-Agent, CT & OLG Models

Models with uninsured idiosyncratic income risk, borrowing constraints, wealth distributions, finite lifetimes, and firm/bank heterogeneity live across frdc hadsge (HouseholdSystem general sequence-space and Reiter/Krusell–Smith models) and the six specialized heterogeneous/overlapping/corporate/banking families under dsge: ct (continuous-time Huggett/Aiyagari/two-asset HANK), olg (Blanchard perpetual-youth OLG), dcegm (discrete-continuous EGM), lifecycle (finite-horizon incomplete-markets life cycle), firm (Khan–Thomas non-convex investment), and bank (heterogeneous banks). See MacroEconometricModels.jl — Heterogeneous Agents.

Quick Start

Load a HouseholdSystem example via MacroEconometricModels.load_ha_example(:huggett) inside a .jl file, compute its stationary wealth distribution and market-clearing interest rate via hadsge steady-state, and solve general-equilibrium dynamics via sequence-space Jacobians (--method ssj):

<!– capture –>

cat > ha_huggett.jl <<'EOF'
MacroEconometricModels.load_ha_example(:huggett)

Sequence-Space HA (hadsge)

Auclert–Bardóczy–Rognlie–Straub sequence-space Jacobians (SSJ) compute linear general-equilibrium impulse responses directly around the stationary cross-sectional distribution $D_{ss}$ without reducing state heterogeneity to moments:

\[H_t(\mathbf{X}, \mathbf{Z}) = 0 \implies d\mathbf{X} = -\mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{X}}^{-1}\mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{Z}} \, d\mathbf{Z},\]

where household blocks propagate backward via Endogenous Grid Method (--hh-solver egm|vfi) and forward along Young (young) or Winberry (winberry) histogram/polynomial distributions (--distribution).

Continuous-Time HA, OLG, Life-Cycle, Firm & Bank Models (dsge ct|olg|...)

Six built-in structural families under frdc dsge require minimal or zero external model files, parameterizing grids and preferences directly via flags:

\[\rho V(a, z) = u(c^*) + \partial_a V(a, z)(r a + w z - c^*) + \sum_{z'\neq z}\lambda_{z z'}\left[V(a, z') - V(a, z)\right].\]

  • Continuous-Time HACT / Two-Asset HANK (dsge ct): Upwind finite-difference HJB + Kolmogorov forward equation (solve, irf, fevd, transition); pass --two-asset and --ge for Kaplan–Moll–Violante liquid/illiquid portfolio dynamics.
  • Perpetual-Youth OLG (dsge olg): Blanchard–Yaari overlapping generations with optional public debt (--debt, survival --gamma) and New Keynesian block (--nk).
  • Discrete-Continuous & Life-Cycle (dsge dcegm, dsge lifecycle): Iskhakov–Rust–Schjerning upper-envelope DC-EGM with retirement/work choice (--work-option), kinks, and finite-horizon overlapping-generations transitions (--j, --j-retire, --no-annuities).
  • Non-Convex Firms & Heterogeneous Banks (dsge firm, dsge bank): Khan–Thomas $(S,s)$ capital adjustment (steady-state, irf, transition) and Bewley heterogeneous-bank partial/general equilibrium (steady-state, pe, irf, transition).

<!– capture –>

frdc dsge olg solve --alpha 0.36 --beta 0.96 --delta 0.08 --gamma 0.98 --debt 0.2

Common Pitfalls

  1. Passing a HouseholdSystem file to dsge solve or vice versa: Representative-agent dsge solve rejects HouseholdSystem models (usage/wrong-command, exit 2) pointing to hadsge solve; conversely hadsge rejects representative-agent @dsge specs.
  2. Keeping default --n-reduced 30 on reiter distribution-IRFs with coarse --t-horizon: Use --method ssj when only aggregate Jacobians and IRFs are required; reserve reiter with Winberry/Young polynomial reduction for distributional/inequality moments and keep grids balanced relative to computing budget.
  3. Mixing --two-asset continuous-time grids with coarse --max-iter: Two-asset Kaplan–Moll–Violante GE loops take substantially more upwind sweeps than one-asset Aiyagari (--two-asset absent); increase --max-iter when checking convergence diagnostics.
