Regression, Systems & GMM/SMM

Continuous cross-sectional, cointegrating, regularized, quantile, nonparametric, multi-equation simultaneous, and moment-based estimators live under estimate regression (reg, iv, qreg, rdd, lasso, ridge, elastic-net, robust, cointreg, kde, kernel-reg, lowess, sur, 3sls, gmm, smm), with fitted predictions (predict regression) and residuals (residuals regression) for reg, sur, and 3sls. (Discrete choice, Tobit, truncated, and Heckman selection estimators are covered in Discrete Choice, Count & Selection.) See MacroEconometricModels.jl — Cross-Sectional & GMM for upstream details.

Quick Start

Simulate an instrumental-variables dataset (lp-iv) with endogenous regressor s, control x2, and excluded instrument z1, then fit 2SLS (--method tsls) with HC1 covariance:

<!– capture –>

friedman --quiet data simulate lp-iv --periods 200 --seed 42 --save-data iv_sim
friedman estimate regression iv iv_sim --dep y --endogenous s --instruments z1 --method tsls --cov-type hc1

Linear OLS, IV/2SLS, Quantile & Treatment Effects (reg, iv, qreg, rdd)

Standard cross-sectional linear and quasi-experimental regressions cover heteroskedasticity-, cluster-, and Conley spatial-HAC inference as well as causal discontinuities:

\[y_i = x_i'\beta + \varepsilon_i, \qquad \widehat{\beta}_{\text{GMM/2SLS}} = (X' P_Z X)^{-1} X' P_Z y.\]

Shrinkage, Robust, Cointegrating & Nonparametric Regressions

  • Penalized Linear Models (lasso, ridge, elastic-net): Select regularization weight --lambda automatically by cross-validation, AIC, BIC, or EBIC (--select cv|aic|bic|ebic; --alpha blends $\ell_1/\ell_2$ penalties).
  • Outlier-Resistant Regression (robust): Computes IRLS M-estimates or high-breakdown MM-estimates using Huber (--psi huber) or Tukey biweight (--psi bisquare) loss (robust_regression_coefficients, robust_regression_diagnostics).
  • Single-Equation Cointegrating Regression (cointreg): Eliminates endogeneity and serial-correlation bias among $I(1)$ variables via Fully Modified OLS (fmols), Canonical Cointegrating Regression (ccr), or Dynamic OLS (dols) with automatic leads/lags and Andrews HAC kernels; validate stability using test park-added and test stability hansen-instability.
  • Nonparametric Curves (kde, kernel-reg, lowess): Estimate univariate kernel densities (silverman/custom bandwidth), Nadaraya–Watson / local linear / local polynomial regressions (--method nw|ll|lp), and robust locally weighted scatter smoothing (--frac, --iter).

Simultaneous Equations & Moment Estimators (sur, 3sls, gmm, smm)

Multi-equation linear systems and generalized method-of-moments / simulated method-of-moments estimators accept concise model card files (--config) or TOML configs defined in GMM, SMM, Systems, State-Space & Policy:

\[\hat{\theta}_{\text{GMM}} = \arg\min_\theta \bar{g}_T(\theta)' W \bar{g}_T(\theta), \qquad \bar{g}_T(\theta) = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^T g(z_t, \theta).\]

  • Seemingly Unrelated & Three-Stage Least Squares (sur, 3sls): Supply an equations: card (plus instruments: or per-equation | instr for 3sls); pass --iterate on sur for iterative Zellner GLS (sur_coefficients, 3sls_coefficients).
  • GMM (gmm): Use gmm lp: cards for horizon-0 moment conditions or gmm iv: cards for linear IV-GMM (--weighting identity|optimal|twostep|iterated); output keys are gmm_estimates and gmm_diagnostics.
  • SMM (smm): Matches simulated autocovariance moments (ar1, arp, var1, iid_normal) to sample moments (--sim-ratio, --burn, leading --seed).

<!– capture –>

friedman --quiet data simulate arima --phi 0.6 --sigma 0.8 --periods 150 --burn 50 --seed 9 --save-data ar1_sim
cat > smm_card <<'EOF'
smm:
  model: ar1
  theta0: 0.4, 0.5
  lags: 2
  lower: -0.99, 1.0e-4
  upper: 0.99, 10.0
EOF
friedman --seed 9 estimate regression smm ar1_sim --config smm_card --sim-ratio 4 --burn 50

Common Pitfalls

  1. Passing --set or --config-json alongside a model card file: Overrides merge only into .toml configs; place overrides directly in the card or convert to [gmm]/[smm]/[[equations]] TOML (Model Cards & Config Grammar).
  2. Neglecting id / non-regressor columns (obs) in simulated cross-sections: When running --dep y without formula subsetting on reg, ensure CSV/handles exclude non-explanatory ID indices or use clean predictor columns.
  3. Under-identified smm lag count (var1 or arp): SMM identification requires $k(k+1)/2 + k \cdot \text{lags} \ge \#\theta$; increase lags inside the smm: card when fitting higher-order models.
  4. Conflating weak IVs with valid overidentification: Always inspect first-stage partial F in test iv weak-instrument; when instruments are weak, switch to --method liml or construct test iv anderson-rubin sets instead of 2SLS Wald intervals.
