VAR, BVAR, VECM & Factor Models

Reduced-form and high-dimensional multivariate time-series models capture joint lag feedback, Bayesian shrinkage, long-run cointegration, mixed-frequency ragged edges, drifting parameters, and pervasive factor comovement across estimate multivariate and estimate factor, paired with forecast, predict, and residuals. See MacroEconometricModels.jl — VAR & VECM and Factor Models.

Quick Start

Import the five-series Danish money demand panel (:denmark), fit a reduced-form VAR(2) saving the model handle, and compute a 4-step analytical forecast without re-estimating:

<!– capture –>

friedman --quiet data import :denmark -o macro
friedman --seed 42 --quiet estimate multivariate var macro --lags 2 --save-model var_fit
friedman forecast multivariate var --model var_fit.jld2 --horizons 4 --ci-method analytical

Reduced-Form & Bayesian VARs (var, bvar, mfvar, tvpvar, scenario)

A vector autoregression of order $p$ models an $N \times 1$ endogenous vector $y_t$ jointly across its own lags:

\[y_t = c + A_1 y_{t-1} + \dots + A_p y_{t-p} + u_t, \qquad u_t \sim (0, \Sigma).\]

  • Frequentist VAR (estimate multivariate var): Estimates equation-by-equation OLS (--lags auto-selected by AIC when omitted), outputting var_coefficients and information_criteria. Check lag criteria and companion eigenvalue stability via chap-tests (test multivariate lagselect, test multivariate stability).
  • Bayesian VAR (estimate multivariate bvar): Combines Gaussian-inverse-Wishart or Minnesota shrinkage (Priors, SVAR & VECM Identification) with Giannone–Lenza–Primiceri hierarchical optimization (--hyperopt glp|grid) and direct or Gibbs posterior sampling (--sampler direct|gibbs), emitting bvar_coefficients, minnesota_hyperparameters, and information_criteria.
  • Mixed-Frequency BVAR (estimate multivariate mfvar): Bridges high-frequency monthly indicators with low-frequency quarterly targets (--low-freq, --freq-ratio 3, --aggregation growth) via state-space Kalman sampling (--prior minnesota|diffuse).
  • Time-Varying Parameter VAR (estimate multivariate tvpvar): Primiceri drifting-coefficient VAR with stochastic volatility (disable via --no-tvp or --no-sv; tune priors --k-q, --k-s, --k-w).
  • Conditional Scenarios (forecast multivariate scenario): Computes Waggoner–Zha conditional paths (conditional_forecast, implied_structural_shocks) given --conditions-file and --method var|bvar.

Cointegrated Systems (vecm)

When $I(1)$ series share $r < N$ stationary linear equilibrium relations $\beta' y_t$, the Granger Representation Theorem rewrites the level VAR($p$) in error-correction form:

\[\Delta y_t = \Pi y_{t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} \Gamma_i \Delta y_{t-i} + c_0 + c_1 t + \varepsilon_t, \qquad \Pi = \alpha \beta',\]

where $\beta$ ($N \times r$) holds cointegrating vectors and $\alpha$ ($N \times r$) measures error-correction adjustment speeds.

estimate multivariate vecm supports Johansen trace/eigenvalue MLE (--method johansen, automatic rank selection via --rank auto and --significance) as well as single-equation engle_granger, configurable deterministics (--deterministic none|constant|trend), and full post-estimation (forecast, predict, residuals, irf, fevd, hd vecm) plus likelihood-ratio hypothesis tests under test vecm.

Factor & Factor-Augmented Models (factor, favar)

High-dimensional panels of macroeconomic series can be compressed into $r \ll N$ latent factors $F_t$ capturing pervasive comovement before feeding VARs:

\[X_t = \Lambda F_t + e_t, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} F_t \\ Y_t \end{bmatrix} = \Phi_0 + \sum_{\ell=1}^p \Phi_\ell \begin{bmatrix} F_{t-\ell} \\ Y_{t-\ell} \end{bmatrix} + u_t.\]

Common Pitfalls

  1. Supplying $I(1)$ levels directly to BVAR vs VECM without transformation: Use estimate multivariate vecm when testing equilibrium cointegrating vectors $\beta$ on levels; if fitting a large reduced-form VAR or factor model (:fred_md), apply FRED transformations first (Data Management & Simulation).
  2. Omitting --key-vars or --low-freq: estimate multivariate favar needs observable slow/policy series listed in --key-vars (comma-separated names or 1-based indices), and mfvar requires --low-freq alongside --freq-ratio.
  3. Confusing forecast and irf horizons/CI options: Conditional forecast multivariate var takes --ci-method analytical|bootstrap|none with --confidence 0.95, whereas structural irf var uses --ci and --replications (Chapter Structural ID, LP & Innovation Accounting).
