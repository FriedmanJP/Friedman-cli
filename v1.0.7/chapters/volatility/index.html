Volatility & Multivariate GARCH

Financial and macroeconomic volatility clustering, leverage asymmetries, long-memory persistence, mixed-frequency macro drivers, latent stochastic volatility, and time-varying conditional covariance matrices are handled across all 14 volatility subcommands under estimate volatility, alongside conditional variance prediction (predict volatility), standardized residuals (residuals volatility), and multi-step volatility forecasting (forecast volatility). See the upstream Volatility manual in MacroEconometricModels.jl.

Quick Start

Simulate a 250-period GARCH(1,1) series with a fixed seed, estimate conditional variance parameters (Gaussian innovations), and forecast conditional variance across a 6-step horizon:

<!– capture –>

friedman --quiet data simulate garch --kind garch --periods 250 --seed 42 --save-data garch_sim
friedman --quiet estimate volatility garch garch_sim --p 1 --q 1 --save-model garch_fit
friedman forecast volatility garch --model garch_fit.jld2 --horizons 6

Univariate GARCH & Asymmetric Volatility

Conditional heteroskedasticity models express demeaned innovations $r_t = \mu + \varepsilon_t$ as $\varepsilon_t = \sigma_t z_t$ where $z_t$ follows normal, Student-$t$, or generalized error distributions (--dist normal|student|ged). Standard GARCH($p,q$) updates conditional variance $\sigma_t^2$, whereas GJR-GARCH adds asymmetric indicator responses $\mathbb{I}\{\varepsilon_{t-i} < 0\}$ and EGARCH models log variance directly without non-negativity restrictions:

\[\sigma_t^2 = \omega + \sum_{i=1}^q \left(\alpha_i + \gamma_i \mathbb{I}_{\varepsilon_{t-i}<0}\right)\varepsilon_{t-i}^2 + \sum_{j=1}^p \beta_j \sigma_{t-j}^2, \qquad \ln \sigma_t^2 = \omega + \dots\]

Integrated, Long-Memory, MIDAS & Stochastic Volatility

Volatility shocks often decay hyperbolically rather than exponentially, obey unit persistence ($\alpha + \beta = 1$), depend on low-frequency macro drivers, or evolve via latent log-volatility innovations $h_t$:

\[(1 - \beta(L))(\ln \sigma_t^2 - \dots) \quad \text{vs.} \quad h_{t+1} = \mu + \phi (h_t - \mu) + \eta_t, \quad \eta_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_\eta^2).\]

  • Integrated & Long-Memory Volatility: igarch constrains persistence to unity; figarch and fiegarch introduce fractional differencing $(1-L)^d$ with initial --d0 0.4 and lag truncation --truncation 1000.
  • MIDAS Long-Run Decomposition (garch-midas): estimate volatility garch-midas multiplicatively splits short-run GARCH dynamics from a secular component driven either by realized return blocks (--rv realized) or by an exogenous macro driver [garch_midas] x_lf passed via --config (GMM, SMM, Systems, State-Space & Policy).
  • Bayesian Stochastic Volatility (sv): estimate volatility sv estimates the Kim–Shephard–Chib MCMC sampler (--draws) using leading --seed for reproducibility (sv_coefficients, sv_conditional_variance, sv_standardized_residuals).

<!– capture –>

friedman --quiet --seed 42 data simulate sv --mu -0.5 --phi 0.95 --sigma-eta 0.2 --periods 150 --save-data sv_sim
friedman --seed 42 estimate volatility sv sv_sim --draws 400

Multivariate GARCH (ccc, dcc, bekk)

Multivariate volatility models decompose the conditional covariance matrix $H_t = D_t R_t D_t$ into univariate GARCH volatilities $D_t = \mathrm{diag}(\sqrt{h_{1t}}, \dots, \sqrt{h_{Nt}})$ and constant (ccc) or time-varying (dcc) conditional correlation $R_t$, or specify quadratic covariance updates directly in bekk:

\[Q_t = (1 - a - b)\bar{Q} + a (\tilde{\varepsilon}_{t-1}\tilde{\varepsilon}_{t-1}') + b Q_{t-1}, \qquad R_t = \mathrm{diag}(Q_t)^{-1/2} Q_t \mathrm{diag}(Q_t)^{-1/2}.\]

  • estimate volatility ccc: Constant Conditional Correlation GARCH(--p, --q).
  • estimate volatility dcc: Engle's Dynamic Conditional Correlation (--correction none|aielli for cDCC consistency).
  • estimate volatility bekk: Positive-definite BEKK covariance recursion (--kind scalar|diagonal).

friedman --quiet data simulate var --periods 300 --burn 50 --seed 2 --save-data mv_sim

Common Pitfalls

  1. Omitting --config [garch_midas] when --rv macro is set: Under the default --rv realized, estimate volatility garch-midas requires no config; switching to --rv macro requires a TOML [garch_midas] vector x_lf covering at least floor(n / m_freq) blocks (GMM, SMM, Systems, State-Space & Policy).
  2. Expecting predict/forecast leaves for ccc, dcc, bekk: Conditional covariance prediction and residual extraction live exclusively for the 11 univariate GARCH/SV models (arch, garch, egarch, gjr-garch, aparch, cgarch, figarch, fiegarch, igarch, garch-midas, sv); multivariate GARCH outputs its final conditional correlations directly during estimate.
  3. Re-running sv MCMC without --seed: Stochastic volatility draws depend on RNG state; always fix leading --seed so posterior summaries (sv_coefficients) and model reproduce verify cleanly.
